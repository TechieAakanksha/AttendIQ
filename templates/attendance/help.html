Help center

Quick answers for using AttendIQ effectively and safely.

For students

You can view only your own dashboard and attendance history, filtered by date or subject. Teacher analytics, live scan and the admin center are not available to student accounts.

My attendance is empty

Ask your teacher to add your student account and mark attendance. Sign in with the username and password your teacher shared (the default username is your lowercase roll number).

For teachers

Add subjects, class slots and students, enroll consented face samples, run live scans, mark attendance manually and review analytics.

Setting up a new class

Create the subject (Subjects), add a slot (Class schedule), then add students and enroll their faces (Students).

QR attendance — two ways

A · Teacher scans student passesOpen QR attendance, connect the camera and start scanning. Students show their personal QR (from My QR pass or a printed card). Each student is marked once per session.
B · Students scan the class QROpen Session QR on the projector. Students scan it with their phone camera, sign in, and are marked present. The code refreshes every 30 seconds so screenshots stop working.

Getting the best face-scan results

1 · Enroll wellCapture 5–8 sharp, front-facing frames in even light with slight angle variation.
2 · Scan clearlyPlace the camera at eye level with light on faces. Students should look toward the camera.
3 · Finish the sessionUse End & mark absentees so students who were not scanned are recorded as absent.

Browsers only allow camera access on localhost or HTTPS. Face images never leave your server.
