How enrollment works

Face samples are stored in your own server's media folder and used to train a local OpenCV recognizer. Nothing is sent to a third-party service.

  1. Capture {{ min_samples }}–{{ max_samples }} clear frames (5–8 is ideal).
  2. Face the camera in good, even lighting; remove sunglasses or masks.
  3. Vary the angle slightly: straight, a little left, a little right.
  4. Frames without a detectable face are skipped automatically.
Only enroll students who have given informed consent.
